How to structure VydoraAI scripts so viewers actually buy — CTA placement, script style selection, and the 7 conversion rules that separate channels earning $50/month from channels earning $500+/month.

0:00–0:45
Hook — The Pattern Interrupt
State the specific result or counterintuitive truth immediately. Never start with "Welcome back to my channel." VydoraAI's Monetization style script opens with this automatically.
"Most people trying to lose weight are doing cardio first thing in the morning — and it's actually working against them. Here's why, and what to do instead."
0:45–1:30
Problem — Agitate the Pain
Name the exact frustration the viewer is experiencing. Be specific — not "it's hard" but "you've tried X and Y and still get Z." This is why they keep watching.
"You've spent hours researching, you've started 3 different plans, and every time you make progress you hit the same wall. The issue isn't your willpower."
1:30–2:30
Solution Preview — Earn Their Trust
Introduce the mechanism (not the product) that solves the problem. This is why the Authority and Monetization styles outperform Viral for affiliate income — they establish credibility before selling.
"There's a metabolic window most people aren't using. I'll show you the exact 12-minute protocol that changes this — no equipment, no gym, just timing."
CTA #1
~2:30
First Affiliate CTA — 40% Mark (Critical)
Place the affiliate mention here. Viewers who reach 2:30 in a 6-minute video are engaged — this is your highest-converting moment. VydoraAI's Monetization style places this automatically. Keep it soft: "I'll leave the link below if you want to explore this further."
"If you want a done-for-you version of this protocol, the program I used is linked in the description — it's $37 right now during launch week."
2:30–4:30
Proof + Deep Dive
Demonstrate the mechanism works. This is where most AI scripts generate thin content — add one specific data point, case study, or documented result. For faceless channels, use the result from the JV page or documented outcomes from the niche.
"In a 2023 study from the NIH — I'll link to it in the description — participants using this protocol lost 2.3× more fat than the control group in the same 8-week period."
4:30–5:30
The Step-by-Step (What They Came For)
Deliver the actionable content clearly. Use numbered steps — they increase video completion rate because viewers want to see the list finished. VydoraAI's Scene Engine visualizes each step as its own scene.
CTA #2
~5:30
Closing CTA — Harder Ask
After delivering value, make the direct offer. Viewers who reach this point have implicitly said "I trust this creator." This CTA can be more direct: "If you want the complete system, check the link below — I negotiated a discount for my viewers."
"Everything I covered today is the free version. The full system is below — it's $37 during launch and goes up after this week."

VydoraAI Script Style Selection Guide

Monetization
Best for: Finance, Software, Business
Structures content around a problem, solution, and offer. Automatically places affiliate CTA at the 40% engagement peak. Use this for your first 3 videos in any niche — it's the safest converter.
Authority
Best for: Health, Education, Career
Builds credibility before selling. Longer proof sections, data-heavy middle. Lower CTR on first watch but significantly higher return viewer rate — builds the channel's trust faster.
Listicle
Best for: Tools, Tips, Product Reviews
"Top 5 X that do Y" structure. Highest search-snippet appearance. Embed affiliate CTA on item #2 and item #5 — viewers who finish listicles are in purchase research mode.
Viral
Best for: Shorts, Trend Content
Optimized for shares and recommendations, not search. Use for Shorts only. Weak for affiliate conversion — avoid for long-form monetization channels until you have 5K+ subscribers.
Story
Best for: Personal Finance, Health Journeys
Narrative arc: problem → attempt → solution → outcome. Highest viewer retention of all styles. Use after establishing credibility (after video 5+). Requires VydoraAI's Character Lock for avatar consistency across scenes.
